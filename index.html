About

Dr. Sandeep Dalal is a Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Applications at Maharshi Dayanand University, Rohtak, with 15 years of teaching experience. His work spans software testing, software engineering, computer networks, cloud security, IoT and machine learning.

He has served on the U.G. and P.G. Boards of Studies, worked as Training and Placement Officer for the department, and reviews for international conferences and journals. He is a Life Member of the Computer Society of India and the Indian Science Congress Association.

Maharshi Dayanand University is a state university established under Haryana Act No. XXV of 1975 and is an 'A+' grade university accredited by NAAC.

Director, Digital Learning Centre

Prof. Dalal leads the Digital Learning Centre at Maharshi Dayanand University, supporting technology-enabled teaching and learning across the university. His work on e-learning models, AI-based learning architectures and Green-IoT practices informs the Centre's direction.
